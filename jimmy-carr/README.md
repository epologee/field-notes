# Jimmy Carr: We’re at the beginning of a revolution

A chapter reading of Jimmy Carr's conversation with Chris Williamson on Modern Wisdom (10 August 2026). A one-on-one conversation where Jimmy builds longer arguments suits chapters better than posters, so this site uses the same chapter form as the Rick Rubin reading: `chapters.yaml` rendered through `template.html` by `build.py`.

`chapters.yaml` holds 49 chapters in Jimmy's first person, each with a direct quote and links into the video. Rebuild with `python3 build.py`.

## Design: Admit One

Jimmy tours around three hundred nights a year, two shows a night, so the palette comes from the ticket rather than the stage.

| Role | Colour | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Page | `#c8dbcd` | cloakroom and raffle ticket stock comes in green, pink, blue, white and buff |
| Chapter card | `#fdfdfb` | the white thermal paper of box-office tickets printed on BOCA printers |
| Text | `#1c2230` | blue-black thermal print |
| Accent | `#d1242f` | the red of Admit One roll tickets |
| Numbers and timecodes | `#4b3fa0` | violet date-stamp ink |
| Chapter strips | `#f2b8c6` `#a9c8ec` `#f3dc7a` `#9fd3ab` | theatre tickets coloured by seating area: pink, blue, yellow, green |

Each chapter card carries one narrow strip in a seating colour and a quiet perforation line; there are no notches, stamps or ticket puns. Headings use Archivo at narrow widths, like a theatre board; body text uses Literata; numbers and timecodes use IBM Plex Mono. The table of contents is called Set list, which is what comedians call it.
